## Review corrections (2026-10-03)

- The NWH power curve represents normalized kW, with 650 Nm between 1750 and 4500 rpm and 350 kW from 5500 to 6250 rpm. Intermediate/off-plateau values are simulation tuning, not a measured dyno trace.
- Rear differential uses NWH mechanical limited-slip mode. Stiffness/ramp values are simulation tuning. Standard equipment confirmed by Mercedes-Benz USA: https://media.mbusa.com/news/2018-c-class-cabriolet-quick-reference-guide
- Wheel centers retain the published wheelbase, with the requested 70 mm forward shift on both axles and 40 mm inward shift on every corner. Factory track values remain reference data. Seat/steering are on negative vehicle X; front is positive Z.
- The supplied mesh depicts the roof open; the vehicle is configured as unenclosed.
- WashLoop/WashEnd are original deterministic synthesized noise, with no donor sound clips. Sleep balance and wash VFX are authored Mercedes assets.
