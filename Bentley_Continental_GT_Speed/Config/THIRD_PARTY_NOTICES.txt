Engine audio
------------
EngineIdle.wav, EngineLow.wav, EngineLowLoad.wav, EngineMid.wav,
EngineMidLoad.wav, EngineHigh.wav, and EngineHighLoad.wav are unchanged copies
of the existing synthetic V8 loops in Assets/Mods/McLaren_Senna/Config/Audio.
They are reused from the repository under the MIT license in SDK_LICENSE.txt.
The Bentley mod does not synthesize, record, or process engine audio.

Native wash audio
-----------------
Audio/BentleyWashLoop.wav and Audio/BentleyWashEnd.wav are unchanged byte
copies of the SDK's Example-Vehicle/Audio/CarWashLoop.wav and
CarWash_Ending.wav, the clips used by the canonical vehicle wash setup.
They are reused from the repository under the MIT license in SDK_LICENSE.txt.
The Bentley wash setup no longer synthesizes or processes wash audio.
Original SDK asset GUIDs (provenance only):
loop 7ee76d1b2ed0d21439cb83b2e65dc2f8
ending c46893eb4eb22fe4fad4703f2ce50c98
The Bentley-owned asset GUIDs are preserved; no Example-Vehicle mod is required.
