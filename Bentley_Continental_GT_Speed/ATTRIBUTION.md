## Changes

The supplied model was scaled to the game's vehicle dimensions (4.895 m body length) and converted into target-owned Unity meshes and materials. Its body paint zones, trim, glass, interior, and wheel materials were adapted for the game's renderer and repaint flow. The model was integrated with the game's vehicle rig, wheel, collider, and damage lifecycle.
