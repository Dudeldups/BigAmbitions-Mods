[h1]Porsche 911 GT3 RS[/h1]

Less luxury cruiser, more road-legal race car. The Porsche 911 GT3 RS is made for players who want something fast, sharp and unapologetically performance-focused.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Vehicle Specs[/h2]

[b]Type:[/b] Road-legal race car
[b]Top Speed:[/b] 296 km/h
[b]0–100 km/h:[/b] 3.2 seconds
[b]Cargo Capacity:[/b] 2 slots
[b]Dealer:[/b] Manhattan Luxury Cars and The Hamptons Axis
[b]Price:[/b] $250,000

Acceleration and braking are tuned to closely match the real-world vehicle.

The car can be registered with the private driver services.
