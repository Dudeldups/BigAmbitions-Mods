Acceleration validation stops at throttle/brake interruption, steering, lost
wheel contact or the first collision (158.347 s). Braking distances interpolate
the threshold crossing; endpoints <=1 km/h approximate a stop. The ~0.2 s
sampling interval introduces additional measurement uncertainty. Initial
neutral selection at near-zero road speed is accepted as normal launch.

## Applied calibration

- Absolute wheel/controller/caliper pivots: front z=1.155 m, rear z=-1.515 m;
  another 80 mm rearward, retaining the 2.670 m wheelbase and current tracks.
- Wheel contact/visual centres raised 30 mm relative to the chassis pivot:
  front y=0.38705 m, rear y=0.40850 m. With tyre sizes/springs preserved, this
  lowers the supported chassis by approximately 30 mm rather than moving
  body visuals through their physical colliders at runtime.
- Shared engine curve: 588 kW at 7250 RPM, no interpolated torque above
  800 Nm, 800 Nm plateau at 5500..6700 RPM. Intermediate turbo-spool values
  are game calibration; McLaren does not publish a full dyno trace here.
- Native engine modifier models 85% net driveline efficiency. NWH already
  compensates its internal engineLossPercent at full throttle, so changing
  that field alone would not provide wheel-power losses. Real mass/power,
  tyre dimensions, gearing and the 335 km/h speed limit are retained.
- Upshift 8000 RPM, downshift 4500 RPM, post-shift ban 0.18 s. First-to-second
  now drops to ~5248 RPM, safely above the downshift threshold. The former
  5500 threshold crossed that drop and caused repeated 1/2 reversals.
- Native brake modifier: 2100 Nm below 100 km/h, progressive speed-squared
  response to a 2800 Nm cap at/above 200 km/h. This approximates additional
  Senna aero braking without altering grip/steering. Targets use 1.25 times
  factory deceleration: 29.5/1.25=23.6 m and 100/1.25=80 m, not 25% more than
  the already excessive installed brake torque. This response remains a
  candidate calibration until another straight CSV validates both stops.
- Engine loudness/load crossfade unchanged. Carrier matches the previous
  50..320 Hz curve through 6000 RPM; above it rolls off monotonically to
  220 Hz instead of 320 Hz at 8500 RPM. Horn and existing clips unchanged.
- Clear front/rear lamp-cover triangles split from the imported cabin-window
  mesh, retaining coordinates and UVs. Clear and red lenses own damage
  clones; cabin glass is excluded. Rear dent height reaches the spoiler.
  Changed meshes recalculate normals/tangents; repair restores authored data.

## Verification and remaining runtime work

Unity fixtures exercise exact controller/rolling/caliper positions, power
and torque bounds, shift hysteresis, native modifier deduplication/removal,
native zero-RPM restart safety, upper audio compression, front/rear lens and
spoiler deformation, shared-source isolation and repair of mesh bindings,
vertices, normals and tangents. Stored and bundled prefabs are checked.
This does not establish achieved in-game acceleration or braking times.

After installing/restarting: drive straight from rest to 100 and 200 with
continuous full throttle and no steering; brake from above 100/200 to rest
without steering/handbrake. Repeat 0-100 at least three times. Include one
stop/relaunch, reverse, exit/re-entry and repair/relaunch. Check road contact,
upper-RPM sound, front/rear lights-on impacts and spoiler deformation. Send
that CSV for the next measured correction; do not mark performance complete
until the installed build meets the agreed targets in game.
