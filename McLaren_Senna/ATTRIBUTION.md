The supplied GLB is redistributed and adapted for this Big Ambitions vehicle mod under CC BY 4.0. The conversion adjusts the model orientation and dimensions, fits the four authored wheel groups to the factory tyre sizes, assigns target-owned materials, and adds runtime lighting, damage, physics, paint, and vehicle lifecycle integration. Engine and horn audio are synthesized for this mod; no recorded third-party audio is included.

The dedicated Senna idle loop is procedurally synthesized from periodic firing harmonics and exhaust modulation by `tools~/generate_audio.py`; it contains no sampled or recorded third-party sound. Wheel calibration preserves the supplied model attribution and texture atlas.
