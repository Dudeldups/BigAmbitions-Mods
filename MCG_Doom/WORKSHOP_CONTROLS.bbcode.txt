[h2]Controls[/h2]

Because Big Ambitions / More Computer Games reserves some keys, two original DOOM controls are remapped:

[b]P[/b] - Open the DOOM menu
[b]E[/b] - Toggle the automap

[b]Esc[/b] - Big Ambitions pause menu
[b]Backspace[/b] - Return to the computer game selection
[b]Tab[/b] - Leave the computer

Standard DOOM controls such as movement, firing, using doors/switches and weapon selection work normally.
