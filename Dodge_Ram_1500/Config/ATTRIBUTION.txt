Changes for this mod: the source GLB was imported and rescaled to the published
vehicle dimensions. Its shared wheel, rim, caliper, and light meshes were split
or adapted into independently controlled vehicle parts. The source materials
and geometry were otherwise retained where practical.

All Dodge Ram 1500 TRX engine and horn clips are original deterministic
mathematical synthesis; they do not contain recorded or third-party samples.

Lamp-overlay update: complete-face selections supplied by the user in
DodgeRamLightOverlays.blend, derived from the same Ddiaz Design model above.
The extracted material-free overlays retain the original model attribution
and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. Export and production-topology validation are
documented in VEHICLE_SPECS.md and Models/DodgeRamLightSelections.json.

Crash01.wav through Crash07.wav: unchanged Car_Crash_01_alt2.wav through
Car_Crash_07_alt2.wav from the SDK Example-Vehicle/Audio assets (Hovgaard Games).
Copied into this mod with independent Unity asset GUIDs for standalone bundling.
Repository MIT license: SDK_LICENSE.txt. Native NWH playback remains in the game;
no third-party plugin code or binaries are redistributed.
