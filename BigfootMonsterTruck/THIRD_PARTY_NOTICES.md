The vehicle model used by this mod is **“Bigfoot”** by **Superfamicom**.

The unmodified downloaded GLB and its original attribution file are retained in
`Source~/` for traceability. The in-game model is a processed derivative that
separates the four wheels and assigns transparent windshield material while
preserving the original geometry and textures.
