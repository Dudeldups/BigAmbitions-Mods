[h1]Bigfoot Monster Truck[/h1]
[b]Why wait behind traffic when you can just drive over it?[/b]

The [b]Bigfoot Monster Truck[/b] brings a slightly less civilized way of getting around the city. It's huge, it's loud, and yes – you can actually drive over other vehicles.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A fully driveable Bigfoot Monster Truck.
[*]Massive tires that can actually climb and drive over other vehicles.
[*]Loud. Very loud. I mean, it's a monster truck.
[*]Comes with the unmistakable [b]TOOT horn[/b] for those rare occasions when driving over traffic isn't enough.
[*]Available from [b]General US Trucks[/b].
[*]Purchase price: [b]$345,000[/b].
[/list]

[h2]Parking[/h2]
Parallel parking is not supported.
Not because of a technical limitation.
[b]Because it simply doesn't fit.[/b]

For normal parking you'll need [b]two parking spaces[/b] – and yes, you'll also pay [b]twice the parking fee[/b].
Consider it the Bigfoot tax.
