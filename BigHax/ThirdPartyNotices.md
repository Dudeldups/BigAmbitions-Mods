# Third-party icon notices

Big Hax embeds small rasterized brand marks solely to identify external-link
buttons.

## Steam

The Steam mark was derived from **Steam icon logo.svg**, attributed to Steam and
published on Wikimedia Commons as simple geometry in the public domain. Steam
and the Steam logo are trademarks of Valve Corporation.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Steam_icon_logo.svg
- Steam branding guidance: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding

## Discord

The Discord symbol was rasterized from Discord's official white Symbol asset.
Discord permits its color, black, and white logo variants under its published
brand guidelines. Discord and the Clyde logo are trademarks of Discord Inc.

- Source and brand guidance: https://discord.com/branding
