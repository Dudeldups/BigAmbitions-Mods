[h1]Big Hax[/h1]

Let’s be honest: sometimes life as a business tycoon is just too much honest work. Enter [b]Big Hax[/b] – a lightweight cheat mod designed to cut the corners you didn't want to walk around anyway.

You're welcome to make suggestions about what other Hax you need.
