# Audi R8

Audi R8 V10 performance quattro: 5.2-litre naturally aspirated V10,
456 kW / 620 PS, 580 Nm, seven-speed S tronic, quattro, 331 km/h,
manufacturer 0–100 km/h in 3.1 seconds.
[Audi press information](https://uploads.audi-mediacenter.com/system/production/uploaded_files/15424/file/bd4525980a25ef6842809522c47238ee9a688973/Press-Information_Audi_R8.pdf?1567165940=&disposition=attachment).

The 12 October 2026 CSV calibration targets realistic acceleration and a
100–0 km/h stop of 26.4 m: the instrumented 33.0 m baseline divided by 1.25,
as requested for 25% stronger braking.
[AMS measured data](https://www.auto-motor-und-sport.de/test/audi-r8-v10-performance/technische-daten/).
The resulting new in-game performance still requires a fresh CSV test.
